HOW IT WORKS

Local AI Video Inpainting in Three Steps

Unlike traditional web tools that upload your files to remote cloud servers, FourFrame processes everything right inside your browser using hardware acceleration.

01 / DECODE

Client-Side Video Decoding

Your video is decoded directly within browser memory using WebAssembly. No bytes are sent over the network, ensuring zero data loss and absolute privacy for sensitive footage.

02 / DETECT

AI Watermark Localization

Automated neural detection pinpoints watermark boundaries, semi-transparent overlays, and text badges across all video frames with sub-pixel localization accuracy.

03 / INPAINT

Neural Pixel Reconstruction

Our deep learning model seamlessly fills in masked watermark regions with spatio-temporal pixel consistency, restoring the natural background without blur or halos.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions about how FourFrame cleans video watermarks privately? Here are the answers.

Are my videos ever uploaded to a server?
Never. FourFrame executes all AI inference and video rendering locally on your machine via WebAssembly (WASM) and ONNX Runtime Web. Your video never leaves your computer, making it 100% compliant with strict enterprise and personal privacy standards.
What video formats and file sizes are supported?
FourFrame supports standard video containers including MP4, MOV, and WebM, as well as high-resolution images (PNG, JPG). Files up to 2 GB in size can be processed directly inside standard modern desktop browsers.
How does AI inpainting compare to conventional blur removal?
Traditional tools simply apply a Gaussian blur or mosaic distortion, which creates an obvious smudge over your video. FourFrame uses deep convolutional neural networks to reconstruct the lost pixels, textures, and gradient flows behind the watermark for a clean, natural finish.
Can FourFrame remove Google Gemini and Veo watermarks?
Yes! FourFrame has built-in detection profiles specialized for Gemini video badges, Veo text badges, and transparent corner logos, with automatic bounding box alignment and alpha gain calibration.
Is FourFrame free to use?
Yes, FourFrame is completely free. There are no subscriptions, account sign-ups, file download limits, or surprise fees.
